## Hypothesis (directional)

Different representations of the same commercial domain (HTML, HTML+JSON-LD, Markdown, Decision Packet) produce measurable differences in **exposure** and labeled **understanding** across agent families and crawlers. We do **not** preregister a ranking or SEO outcome.

## Primary metric

For labeled runs (product_probe / organic paste-back):

1. `exposure.fetch_success` by `variant` × `origin` × `agent_family`
2. Labeled `understanding.entity` and mean `capabilities_score` / `constraints_score` when present

Secondary: citation presence; action occurrence (expected rare).
